### [Shipping JPEG XL in Chrome](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49991227)

- **HN engagement:** 12 points, 1 comments
- **Primary topic:** Chrome shipping JPEG XL decoding support via a Rust-based decoder
- **Evidence basis:** source-and-discussion
- **External page:** success
- **Source:** https://developer.chrome.com/blog/jpeg-xl-in-chrome

Chrome shipping JPEG XL with a memory-safe Rust decoder is a significant codec-standardization milestone that affects how web developers can serve images, especially for high-fidelity or lossless photographic content. The Rust reimplementation approach also signals a broader browser-security pattern of replacing C++ decoders with memory-safe alternatives without sacrificing SIMD performance.

**Linked source:** The Chrome team's blog post announces decoding support for JPEG XL starting in Chrome 155, highlighting that the decoder was reimplemented in Rust (jxl-rs) for memory safety, with SIMD performance parity achieved via a custom abstraction layer and stabilization of the `target_feature_11` Rust feature. The source argues the decision was driven by consistent developer feedback and participation in the Interop 2026 JPEG XL Investigation.

**HN discussion:** The sole HN comment is a reference to the XKCD 'standards' comic (927), implying skepticism about yet another image format competing with existing ones. No technical discussion or developer experience claims appeared in this thread.

Developers may be interested in Nibnib for its highly efficient decompression which can be beneficial in performance-critical environments, particularly where hardware constraints limit the use of more complex formats like JPEG.

**Linked source:** The Nibnib image format focuses on image compression at 4 bits-per-pixel, optimizing for high decompression speeds, mainly for use on microcontrollers like the RP2350. Its palette-based compression improves over conventional methods by storing delta values which enhance soft regions, using a method that decodes images in pixel pairs to achieve rapid performance.

**HN discussion:** HN commenters discussed the positioning of Nibnib's decompression speed compared to formats like JPEG, and highlighted potential inefficiencies of optimizing for specific hardware like the RP2350 over modern processors. Some users noted the historical resemblance to Amiga's HAM video mode and expressed interest in the new language, symbolasm, used in Nibnib's decoder.
